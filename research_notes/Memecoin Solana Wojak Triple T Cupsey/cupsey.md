# CUPSEY (Solana) — Token Profile, as of 8 October 2026

Research method note: every data site (CoinGecko, Phantom, DexScreener, MEXC, BeInCrypto, Bitget, memecoin.wiki, Meme Insider) was blocked by the egress proxy, so all figures below come from search-engine snippets of those pages. Each figure carries the date the snippet showed (or "undated" / "page age N days" where that is all that was available). Figures from different aggregators disagree, sometimes by 100x, because they track DIFFERENT contracts under the same name; this is flagged throughout.

## Q1. What exactly is CUPSEY on Solana — contract, launch date, launchpad, origin, relation to the trader Cupsey, who runs the community?

### Takeaway
"CUPSEY" is a pump.fun-launched Solana memecoin built around a plush cup toy ("Cupsey") that is marketed as the unofficial pump.fun mascot. The dominant token as of October 2026 is the migrated/relaunched contract **6NwarBvDkXhByqVp2Qkq5i9XbtA2B3Bwe8SWGu9vpump** (created on pump.fun in January 2026, ~$6–7M market cap in early Oct 2026); the original contract **5PsnNwPmMtsGZgG6ZqMoDJJi28BR5xpAotXHHiQhpump** (launched ~3 Nov 2024, listed on Gate.io 4 Nov 2024, ATH ~$0.021 on 16 Nov 2024) is now labelled "CUPSEY [OLD]" and trades at ~$45–65K. The token is a community/fan asset two steps removed from the trader: the plushie was named after the trader, the token after the plushie; no source shows the trader @Cupseyy launching, endorsing or holding it. **This note profiles the 6Nwar… contract, with the 5PsnN… contract as its history.**

### Inferences
- The token is best described as a "fan/mascot" memecoin: trader → plushie → token. Its narrative leans on three borrowed reputations (trader Cupsey, pump.fun/Alon, Orangie), none of which formally owns or runs it.
- The January 2026 "migration" looks like a relaunch by the same community team (same @CupseyToken account dating from Nov 2024, "cupseytoken" wallet as top holder on the new mint); old-contract holders appear to have been compensated via the claimed 14% airdrop, but this is unverified.
- The ~16.8% "cupseytoken" holding on pump.fun plus the claimed 8% to KOLs means roughly a quarter of supply was, at some point, under team/insider control (unverified timing; may have since been distributed/sold).

### Gaps
- No source explains WHY the contract migrated in January 2026 (no hack, rug or dispute was reported; the sources simply record the migration).
- The on-chain creator wallet of 6Nwar… and the exact creation timestamp were not retrievable (Solscan blocked).
- Whether the 14% airdrop and 8% KOL allocations actually happened could not be verified on-chain.
