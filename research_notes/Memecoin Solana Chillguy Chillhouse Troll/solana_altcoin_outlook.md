## Key Question 6: Do previous-cycle memecoins reclaim ATHs in the next rally? (Rotation evidence, incl. CHILLGUY, TROLL, CHILLHOUSE history)

### Takeaway
The dominant pattern is replacement, not recovery: DOGE and SHIB never reclaimed 2021 highs in 2024 (DOGE peaked ~$0.48 vs $0.74 ATH; SHIB ~$0.0000475 vs $0.0000885), and the Solana class of 2024 (POPCAT, PNUT, MOODENG, GOAT, FARTCOIN, CHILLGUY) remained 80–97% below their highs through the 2025 and 2026 bounces.
