**Method note / data-access caveat.** The research sandbox's egress proxy blocked direct access to CoinGecko (site and API), CoinMarketCap, DexScreener, Coinbase, Phantom, Kraken, Coinranking, Messari and all news sites. All figures below were therefore recovered via web-search result snippets of those pages (each snippet carries its own "as of" date, which is reproduced per row). The report writer should treat the ranked table as "early October 2026, ±2 weeks" and the percentages as approximations (±10–20% relative error from date mismatch).

## Key Question 1: Total market cap of Solana memecoins and of all memecoins (October 2026)

### Takeaway
As of early October 2026 the Solana memecoin category (CoinGecko "Solana Meme Coins") is worth roughly **$3.4–3.9 billion**, down from ~$4.0–4.5B in spring/early 2026; the entire memecoin sector is ~**$35 billion** (CoinGecko meme category, 6 Oct 2026), i.e. ~77% below the $150.6B peak of December 2024. Solana memes are therefore only ~10% of all memecoin market cap, with Dogecoin alone (~$14.7B) worth 4× the whole Solana meme category.

### Inferences
- Solana memes ≈ $3.4–3.9B ÷ all memes ≈ $35B → Solana memecoins are **~10–11% of the memecoin sector by market cap** (own calculation).
- DOGE ($14.7B) alone is ~42% of the meme category (own calculation from crypto.news figures).
- The $3.40B (18 Sep) vs $3.76B (early Oct) spread is consistent with the modest early-October rally seen in PENGU, BONK and USELESS; the trend since April 2026 (−10–20%) is down.
- Note that CoinGecko's "Solana Meme" category total (~$3.4–3.9B) is clearly **smaller than PUMP's market cap alone (~$1.65B) plus TRUMP+PENGU**, which implies the category does **not** include PUMP (classified as a launchpad token) and likely excludes some multi-chain tokens (e.g. SPX6900, Ethereum-native). ## Key Question 6: Dominant Solana memecoin narratives now and how CHILLGUY/CHILLHOUSE/TROLL fit
