## Key Question 1: What exactly is CHILLHOUSE on Solana — contract, launch date, launchpad, meme origin, creator?

### Takeaway
The token the crypto community means by "CHILLHOUSE" is the Solana SPL token **Chill House (ticker CHILLHOUSE), mint `GkyPYa7NnCFbduLknCfBfP7p8564X1VZhwZYJ6CZpump`**, launched on pump.fun in April 2025 as a parody mash-up of the "Chill Guy" TikTok meme and the Solana memecoin Housecoin ($HOUSE). It has no named founder, no website, no roadmap; its public voice is the X account @ChillHouseSOL. It is NOT tied to Mr. Beast, a "chill house" music genre, or any other trend.
