## Key Question 1 — What exactly is WOJAK on Solana (contract, launch date, launchpad, origin, who runs it, is it a CTO)? Disambiguation vs. other WOJAKs

### Takeaway
The dominant Solana WOJAK is the pump.fun token **`8J69rbLTzWWgUJziFY8jeu5tDwEPBwUz4pKBMr5rpump`** (ticker WOJAK; listed on MEXC as **WOJAKONX**; X: **@WojakOnX**), which graduated its bonding curve on **3 Nov 2025**, hit ~$60M market cap within four weeks, and is run by the @WojakOnX team rather than being a community takeover (CTO). It is unrelated to the April-2023 Ethereum WOJAK (wojakcto.com), to the 2024 "WOJAKSOL" Solana token MEXC listed in June 2024, or to the small Raydium-pair "Wojak" that was community-claimed in April 2025.
