## Key Question 1: In past SOL rallies of +50% or more, what percentage of established Solana memecoins rose, and what was the median and range of returns relative to SOL?

### Takeaway
No source publishes a cross-sectional tally (% up, median, dispersion) of established Solana memecoins per SOL rally window; the evidence is anecdotal per-token. The pattern across windows is: in the 2024 windows the large established memes (WIF, BONK, POPCAT) rose with or more than SOL; in the 2025 windows (Q2–Q3, SOL +24% and +35%) the big memes rallied briefly but ended far below SOL on a multi-month basis (top-10 meme basket −78.7% Jan 2025–Feb 2026 vs BTC −30.7%); in the Jul–Sep 2026 window the Solana meme category was roughly flat (~$3.3–3.9B) while SOL rose ~40–60%, with upside concentrated in brand-new tokens (e.g. ZCAT).

### Inferences
- (Own arithmetic) From Coinlore's quarterly returns, SOL end-Mar 2026 → end-Sep 2026 ≈ (1−0.115)×(1+0.386)−1 ≈ +22.7% (or ~+40–60% if the Digrin monthly closes are right), while the Solana meme category moved from $3.68B (Mar 31) to $3.27B–$3.90B (Sept/Oct), i.e. roughly −11% to +6%. Inference: in the 2026 SOL rally the established-meme basket underperformed SOL by roughly 20–50 percentage points, and the aggregate was propped up by new launches rather than old names.
- In 2024 the established memes had positive beta >1 to SOL (WIF +2,080% vs SOL ~+3x); in 2025 and 2026 the relationship broke on a multi-month horizon: memes rallied sharply for days/weeks in each SOL upswing (July 2025, Sept 2026) but gave back more than SOL on the downswings, so 12-month conditional performance given "SOL up" was still negative for most of the 2024-cohort names.
- The honest cross-sectional base rate from the available evidence: in 2024-type windows most (likely >70%) of established memes rose and the median beat SOL; in 2025–2026 windows the share of established memes up over the full window appears to be a minority, though no source gives the exact count.

## Key Question 2: What is the empirically estimated beta or correlation of Solana memecoins to SOL, and has it changed in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
No Kaiko, Messari, Galaxy or academic source publishes a formal beta of Solana memecoins to SOL. Partial evidence: BONK shows "high beta to SOL" on 30/60/90-day rolling windows (DeFi Report via PANews), with correlation weakening when BONK outperforms; the 2025–26 top-10 meme basket correlated 0.78 with BTC with 104% annualized volatility; qualitative evidence (Kaiko, 21Shares, Galaxy) says the SOL–meme link weakened in 2025–2026 as meme share of Solana volume collapsed and hold times fell to 58–100 seconds.

### Cited Findings
- BONK has a high beta to SOL over 30-, 60- and 90-day rolling windows, but its correlation with SOL tended to weaken during periods when BONK outperformed (data covers on-chain holdings only, excluding ~20% of BONK on CEXs) — [PANews summarizing DeFi Report](https://panews.io/articles/15l9pxk2).
- Equal-weight top-10 memecoin portfolio, Jan 2025–Feb 2026, weekly data: correlation with Bitcoin 0.78, with gold −0.41; annualized volatility 103.82%; max drawdown 82.71% — [Krause 2026 preprint](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/401094962_An_Empirical_Analysis_of_Meme_Coin_Performance_2025-2026). (Beta and Sharpe not in snippets.)
- Cryptonary's beta analysis: memes have higher beta coefficients and lower R-squared than Solana small-cap altcoins — [Cryptonary](https://cryptonary.com/research/forget-alpha-these-beta-plays-will-supercharge-your-portfolio) (date not shown).
- Kaiko KT5 index work: SOL–DOGE return correlation only 0.06; XRP–SOL 0.18 — [Kaiko](https://research.kaiko.com/insights/moving-beyond-bitcoin-with-the-kt5-index). Kaiko separately says SOL "has notably fallen behind" as a high-beta asset with underwhelming 2025 returns and weakening risk-reward — [Kaiko](https://research.kaiko.com/insights/gap-grows-between-bitcoin-and-altcoins).
- 2019–2024 correlation study of 13 memecoins vs BTC/ETH/SOL: memecoins cluster in a separate branch (greater distance to any major than among themselves); PEPE–BONK 0.73, FLOKI–BONK 0.78 — [Medium summary](https://medium.com/@gwrx2005/price-correlation-between-major-cryptocurrencies-and-memecoins-2019-2024-6ce899224366) (secondary, original Gate.io report not verified).
- Low-quality claims: "BONK roughly 85% correlated with SOL" — [Flitpay](https://www.flitpay.com/blog/bonk-price-prediction); "correlation above 0.75 between BONK and Solana network activity" (activity, not price) — [ICOBench](https://icobench.com/news/bonk-price-correlation-solana-beta-proxy/). Neither states a method.
- Structural change markers: median Solana memecoin hold time fell from ~300 s (2024) to ~100 s (Oct 2025) — [CoinDesk on Galaxy report, Oct 1 2025](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2025/10/01/memecoins-are-no-longer-a-joke-galaxy-digital-says-in-new-report); to 58 s in 2026 — [Phemex](https://phemex.com/news/article/solana-memecoin-hold-time-plummets-to-58-seconds-in-2026-70043).
- Memecoin share of Solana spot volume fell from 40% (H1 2025) to 16% (H1 2026) — [21Shares H1 2026 analysis](https://www.21shares.com/en-us/insights/solana-h1-2026-earnings-analysis); [21Shares on X](https://x.com/21shares/status/2095870582508171610).
- "Measuring Memecoin Fragility" (Xiang et al., arXiv 2512.00377, Nov 2025) scores tokens on volatility, whale concentration and sentiment amplification; politically themed tokens (TRUMP, LIBRA) rank most fragile; it does not report SOL beta — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.00377); [Pith review](https://pith.science/paper/2512.00377).
- Academic connectedness work on 2021 (BTC/ETH/DOGE/SHIB, 4-hourly) focuses on spillovers, not SOL beta — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S154461232200397X).

### Inferences
- Taken together: Solana memes behave as a high-beta (>1) asset to SOL in upswings measured over days–weeks, but with low R² — the idiosyncratic (attention/launch-driven) component dominates over months. The 2025–26 evidence (meme basket −79% vs BTC −31%; SOL meme category −85% from peak while SOL was −60% from ATH at Aug 2026 lows, own arithmetic from Coinlore/CoinGecko figures) implies the downside beta has been much larger than the upside beta since 2025.
- The decline in meme share of Solana volume (60%→16–30%) and in hold times suggests the SOL↔meme feedback loop that existed in 2024 (memes drive SOL fees, SOL strength drives meme bids) is structurally weaker in 2026.

### Gaps
- No published numeric beta/correlation of a Solana meme basket vs SOL for any window; PANews/DeFi Report snippet gives direction ("high beta") but not the coefficient.
- Krause paper's beta and Sharpe values not visible in snippets; PDF blocked.

## Key Question 3: After an 80–98% drawdown, what fraction of memecoins achieve 2x, 5x, 10x, or a new ATH within 12 months? What fraction go to zero / lose another 50%?

### Takeaway
The best direct evidence is Coin Metrics (Oct 2026): of 150 CEX-priced Solana memecoins launched since Dec 2023, 81% drew down ≥90% from ATH, only 3.3% (5 tokens) ever reclaimed ATH, and two-thirds never had a second rally at all. No source gives 2x/5x/10x-from-trough conditional rates; the broader universe (Pump.fun) has ~0.2–1.4% graduation and ~98.6% rug/pump-dump classification, so recovery base rates for the full population are far lower than the 3.3%.

### Inferences
- (Own reading) Coin Metrics' 3.3% ATH-reclaim rate is for a survivor-biased sample (CEX-priced) with up to ~2.8 years of observation, so a 12-month-forward ATH-reclaim probability from an 80–98% drawdown is likely ≤3% for established names and far lower for the broad universe.

### Gaps
