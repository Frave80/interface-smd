"Chill Guy" was the dominant TikTok meme of autumn 2024 and peaked in November 2024; Banks asserted copyright, promised takedowns against merchandise and "shitcoins", and has since monetised via licensed products (Youtooz plush, 2025) while never endorsing any token.
