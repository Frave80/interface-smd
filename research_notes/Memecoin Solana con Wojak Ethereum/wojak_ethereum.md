## 4. The January–April 2026 contract migration: why, how, issues

### Takeaway
Announced 31 Jan 2026, the "Great Migration" moved holders from 0x5026 to 0x8De3 via a "flywheel" contract that sold deposited old tokens for ETH, bought the new token on the open market and burned it; it ended 23 Apr 2026 with the remaining wojakmigration.eth balance burned (27.27% of new supply burned). Side effects: CoinEx refused the swap and delisted, Bitget swapped at 1:958 then delisted, the old token's price went to ~zero, and aggregators now carry duplicate/contradictory "WOJAK" listings.

### Cited Findings
- "The migration, announced on 31 January 2026, involved a 'flywheel' mechanism executed via smart contracts. Holders of the old 0x50 $WOJAK token deposited them into a migration contract. The contract would automatically swap those tokens for ETH, use that ETH to buy the new 0x8d $wojak token on the open market, and then permanently burn the newly purchased tokens"; described as a "complex, two-week contract migration designed to consolidate supply and boost scarcity" — [CMC latest updates](https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/wojaknew/latest-updates/); [CMC price prediction page](https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/wojaknew/price-prediction/)
