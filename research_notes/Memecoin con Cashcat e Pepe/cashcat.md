## Key Question 1 — What exactly is CASHCAT: contract, chain, launch date, origin, team; is it the leading meme on Robinhood Chain?

### Takeaway
CASHCAT is an anonymous, community-run, zero-utility ERC-20 memecoin at 0x020bfC650A365f8BB26819deAAbF3E21291018b4 on Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663), launched through the Noxa launchpad in the first days after the chain's 1 July 2026 mainnet; its name references "Cash Cat", the name Robinhood's founders used before "Robinhood". It was the chain's first breakout memecoin and its largest for most of July–August 2026, but was overtaken by Artificial Inu (AI) in early September 2026; it remains a top-two Robinhood Chain meme and is the only one confirmed to be listed in the Robinhood app.
