## Key Question 1: What exactly is TROLL on Solana — contract, launch date, launchpad, origin, who runs the community?

### Takeaway
The dominant "TROLL" is the Solana SPL token with mint 5UUH9RTDiSpq6HKS6bp4NdU9PNJpXRXuiw6ShBTBhgH2, a Pump.fun launch from 2024 (August 2024 per the most detailed account; one aggregator says May 2024) that lay dormant for months, was revived by a community takeover (CTO) in April 2025, and is now run by a pseudonymous team ("Seal"/@Fukupapers as CEO, "Fungi" as CTO) operating as Troll Network Limited, which holds an exclusive crypto/meme-coin license to the Trollface artwork from creator Carlos Ramirez ("Whynne") since September 2025. It must be distinguished from a BSC "Troll Face" token, an Ethereum "TROLL", the old "Trollcoin", "Troll (troll.run)", a PumpSwap "Troll Coin", and a rival Solana Troll token that Ramirez himself briefly promoted on Bags in April 2025.

**Trollface IP / Carlos Ramirez ("Whynne")**
- August 2025: Ramirez told Decrypt he had "no intention" of participating in the surging memecoin, said crypto "diminishes" art, and ruled out legal action; he also said that if he sold his holdings he would crash the coin. Decrypt reported the Troll deployer had earned >$64,000 in SOL via Pump.fun's creator-revenue-sharing feature — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/333881/trollface-creator-crypto-diminishes-art-legal-action-meme-coin); [Bitget summary](https://www.bitget.com/news/detail/12560604899206); [PANews](https://www.panewslab.com/en/articles/db2a941c-bee5-42f3-a8b1-4b25b07b7bcb).

### Inferences
- The project's "official" status rests on a private license, not on Ramirez's endorsement; he explicitly declined to participate and expressed distaste for crypto. The license is a legal/branding moat for CEX listings and merch, not a sign of creator-driven promotion.
- Launch-to-CTO timeline (Aug 2024 dormant → Apr 2025 CTO → Aug 2025 ATH → Sept 2025 IP deal) is typical of "revived old Pump.fun coin" plays; the original deployer appears unaffiliated with the current team.

### Gaps
